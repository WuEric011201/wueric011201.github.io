I'm **Tong Wu**, a Ph.D. student in **Electrical & Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University**, advised by [Dr. Tathagata Srimani](https://www.ece.cmu.edu/directory/bios/srimani-tathagata.html) in the [NEXUS Lab](https://nexus.ece.cmu.edu/). My research is in **3D integrated circuits, ML accelerators, and probabilistic computing**.

Before CMU I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering at **Washington University in St. Louis** (2024), with a minor in Materials Science. I'm supported by the **Dean's Fellowship** and the **Qualcomm Ph.D. Fellowship**.

## Research

- **Ising machine systems** — leading GPU implementation of Ising algorithms and bring-up of a multi-FPGA Ising platform (Xilinx ZCU106 in a ring, Opal Kelly XEM8320 in a mesh); leading a tape-out of a multi-chip Ising accelerator in **TSMC 28 nm** for ultra-large graph problems.
- **Monolithic 3D ICs** — designed and taped out a 1 kb **ReRAM + CNT** memory array with periphery in **Skywater 130 nm BEOL**; validated 1T1R FORM/SET/RESET operations and automated probe-station flows for CNT device characterization.
- **3D SRAM** — 1 kB SRAM with periphery in Cadence Virtuoso hitting ~1 GHz cycle time at TT/LH, with analytical modeling in CACTI and architectural studies in STREAM.

## Industry experience

- **Qualcomm** — Hardware Intern, AI Inference Accelerator Architecture Modeling (Fall 2025). Built workload-aware switch/cluster simulators in Python and C to study NPU-to-switch attachment, multi-hop routing, congestion, multicast, and link utilization.
- **DeepSight Technology** — Electrical Engineer Intern (2022 – 2024). Re-designed an opto-electronic front end, achieving a **60 % noise reduction** and **60 % power saving** (20 – 30 °C drop) through topology selection, PCB layout, and stack-up optimization.

I also write [music and build guitar pedals](/projects/) — see the projects page for the analog and DSP side projects I work on outside of research.
