Hi, I'm **Tong Wu** — an electrical engineer, music producer, and guitarist.

I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from **Washington University in Saint Louis**, with minors in Music and Material Science. My work sits at the intersection of analog circuits, embedded DSP, and music: I love designing guitar pedals, building FPGA-based audio processors, and producing records with the gear I make.

**Experience.** At **Deepsight Technology** (2022–present), I work on photodiode circuit design, testing & verification, laser control automation, ADC data-acquisition automation, and PCB design and layout. Before that I did optoelectronic device fabrication research with the **Chuan Wang Research Group**, and TA'd Electronic Circuits, Electronics Lab, Signals & Systems, and Senior Capstone at WashU.

**Selected projects.** Fuzz & overdrive guitar pedals · 13-band equalizer on FPGA + MicroBlaze · Belton-brick delay + reverb pedal · analog compressor + Teensy multi-effect processor · FM synthesis and VCV-rack analog synth experiments. I also write and record with my college rock band [WatchOUT](https://space.bilibili.com/).

**Publication.** *Inverted Structure of Solution-Processed Organometal Halide Perovskite* — Washington University Open Scholarship.

You can find more of my work on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Qianeric3825), [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/), and my older blog at [nanjingtt.wordpress.com](https://nanjingtt.wordpress.com/).
