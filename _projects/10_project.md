Two VCV Rack patches exploring different synthesis traditions. Original post: [Analog Synthesizer (VCV)](https://nanjingtt.wordpress.com/2023/11/12/vcv-synth-project/).

## West Coast Synthesis

Aimed at something **Éliane Radigue**-like — constantly evolving but over a shorter timescale than her hours-long pieces. Built around an expanded VCV module library and a large mixer-driven patch, routed into Ableton (after some fighting with the audio routing).

## East Coast Synthesis

Smaller patch from September 2022, following the **VCV Cookbook**: classic **VCO → VCF → VCA + ADSR**, with control voltages from a MIDI controller. Three voices:

- **Lead**
- **Bass**
- **Chord**
