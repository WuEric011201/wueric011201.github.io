Ph.D. research at **CMU NEXUS Lab** (Jan 2025 – present), building hardware for combinatorial optimization with Ising machines.

## What we're building

- A **multi-chip Ising machine** in **TSMC 28 nm** with a new architecture that enables ultra-large graph mapping — I lead the team driving the tape-out.
- A **GPU implementation** of the Ising algorithms — I lead the development.
- A **multi-FPGA platform** for bring-up and validation: **Xilinx ZCU106** boards connected in a ring, and **Opal Kelly XEM8320** boards in a mesh.
- A multi-chip Ising machine prototype verified on a **Xilinx ZCU104**.

## Tooling

I also lead the **RTL-to-GDS automation** for probabilistic computing using **Mflowgen**, which orchestrates Cadence **Genus** (synthesis) and **Innovus** (P&R).
