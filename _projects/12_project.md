Ph.D. research at **CMU NEXUS Lab** (Jun 2025 – Oct 2025). A monolithic-3D test chip integrating CMOS logic with **BEOL ReRAM** and **carbon-nanotube FETs** on Skywater 130 nm.

## Tape-out

- Designed a **1 kb ReRAM + CNT memory array** with full peripheral circuitry.
- Targeted **Skywater 130 nm** with **BEOL ReRAM and CNT** stacks.

## Test & validation

- Brought up **1T1R ReRAM arrays** with peripheral support, demonstrating **FORM**, **SET**, and **RESET** operations.
- Automated **probe-station workflows** for CNT device testing — better measurement efficiency and reproducibility.
