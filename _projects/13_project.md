Ph.D. research at **CMU NEXUS Lab** (Sep 2024 – Dec 2024). A custom SRAM design exercise feeding into 3D-memory architectural studies.

## Design

- **1 kB SRAM** with full peripheral circuitry in **Cadence Virtuoso**.
- **1 GHz** cycle time, **≈3800 μm²** area, **2 mW** power at the TT/LH corner.

## Validation & modeling

- Cross-checked design parameters against the analytical memory modeling tool **CACTI**.
- Supported the team in optimizing **3D memory organization** across workloads using the **STREAM** architectural simulator.
