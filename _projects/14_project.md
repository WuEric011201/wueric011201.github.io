Hardware internship at **Qualcomm** (Sep – Dec 2025) on AI Inference Accelerator Architecture Modeling.

## What I built

- **Workload-aware architectural models** for AI inference accelerators with all-to-all interconnects, in **Python** and **C** — enabling quantitative analysis of scaling behavior and system bottlenecks.
- **Cluster- and switch-level simulators** to evaluate NPU-to-switch attachment, multi-hop routing, congestion dynamics, multicast algorithms, and throughput saturation.

## Outcome

- **Optimized switching algorithms** to improve routing efficiency, reduce congestion, and improve overall link utilization.
