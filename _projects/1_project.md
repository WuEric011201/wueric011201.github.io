## Fuzz + overdrive

An op-amp overdrive with **asymmetrical soft clipping**, a **3-band EQ** tone stack shaping the gain stage, and a **clean blend** to keep the low-end fundamental from disappearing into the distortion. Built on a custom 3PDT breakout board.
