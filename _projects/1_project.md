A guitar pedal combining fuzz and overdrive stages. Originally documented on [my WordPress post](https://nanjingtt.wordpress.com/2023/11/14/on-going-guitar-pedal-project/).

## Overdrive section

- Op-amp overdrive circuit with **asymmetrical soft clipping**
- **3-band EQ** tone stack to shape the gain stage
- **Clean blend** circuit to preserve low-end fundamentals

## Build

Custom 3PDT breakout board and a hand-drilled enclosure.
