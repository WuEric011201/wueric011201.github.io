A guitar pedal combining fuzz and overdrive stages, on a custom 3PDT breakout board in a hand-drilled enclosure.

## Overdrive section

- Op-amp overdrive circuit with **asymmetrical soft clipping**
- **3-band EQ** tone stack to shape the gain stage
- **Clean blend** circuit to preserve low-end fundamentals
