A batch of four finished tracks from Fall 2022.

## "Nanjing"

Named after my hometown. Chord progression: **Am6 → Dm9 → G9 → C69**. Murmured vocals that reference street names from home and end with the city's name sung in the local dialect. Some of the harmonic choices ended up unconventional — I'd re-record the vocal in a future pass.
