A collection of five finished tracks: a solo synth-metal instrumental, a song rooted in my hometown, and three collaborations from Fall 2022.

## "WAIT" — Synth metal

A four-minute synth-metal instrumental built with **Dexed**, **Vital**, and **VCV Rack**, influenced by Slipknot and German industrial metal such as Oomph!

Working without lyrics or vocals gave me more structural freedom, but sustaining the ideas across a coherent four-minute arrangement—and writing a solo I was happy with—was the hardest part. It became both my first completed metal piece and my first instrumental track.

## "Nanjing"

Named after my hometown. Chord progression: **Am6 → Dm9 → G9 → C69**. Murmured vocals that reference street names from home and end with the city's name sung in the local dialect. Some of the harmonic choices ended up unconventional — I'd re-record the vocal in a future pass.
