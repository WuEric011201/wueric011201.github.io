A 4-minute synth-metal instrumental built with **Dexed**, **Vital**, and **VCV Rack**, influenced by Slipknot and German industrial metal (think Oomph).

Skipping lyrics and vocals freed me up structurally, but stretching the ideas into a coherent 4-minute arrangement — and writing a solo I was happy with — was the hardest part. First time finishing a full metal piece and my first instrumental song.
