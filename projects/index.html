projects

research

Scalable Ising Machine — Multi-Chip Tape-out & Multi-FPGA Platform

Leading a TSMC 28 nm tape-out of a scalable, multi-chip Ising machine, plus GPU implementation of Ising algorithms and a multi-FPGA platform (ZCU106 ring + XEM8320 mesh) for ultra-large graph combinatorial optimization.

Monolithic 3D IC — ReRAM + Carbon-Nanotube Memory Tape-out

Designed and taped out a 1 kb ReRAM + CNT memory array with periphery in Skywater 130 nm BEOL, validated 1T1R FORM/SET/RESET operations, and automated probe-station flows for CNT device characterization.

3D SRAM Memory Design

1 kB SRAM with periphery in Cadence Virtuoso reaching 1 GHz cycle time at TT/LH with ~3800 μm² area and 2 mW; validated against CACTI and integrated into STREAM workload studies.

AI Inference Accelerator Switch Modeling (Qualcomm)

Workload-aware switch and cluster simulators for AI inference accelerators with all-to-all interconnects — studied NPU-to-switch attachment, multi-hop routing, congestion, multicast, and throughput saturation, then optimized routing for better link utilization.

engineering

Fuzz & Overdrive Guitar Pedal

Op-amp overdrive with asymmetrical soft clipping, 3-band EQ tone stack, and a clean-blend low-end preserve, housed in a hand-drilled enclosure.

13-Band Equalizer on FPGA + MicroBlaze

Stereo 13-band FIR EQ at 50 kHz sample rate, with custom SPI for the LTC1865L ADC and LTC1654 DAC, controlled in C from a MicroBlaze soft-core over AXI4-Lite.

Belton Brick Delay + Reverb Pedal

Hybrid delay-and-reverb pedal built around the PT2399 delay chip and the Belton Brick reverb module.

Analog Compression + Teensy Multi-effect Processor

Analog compression front end combined with Teensy 4.0 DSP — tremolo, delay, and reverb algorithms, line/instrument/mic inputs, and up to 2 W of output to drive a stage cab.

Overdrive Guitar Pedal

A standalone overdrive pedal — Volume, Tone, Gain, and a harshness switch — built for a friend.
